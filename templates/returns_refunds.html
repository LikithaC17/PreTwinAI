🔄 PreTwin Returns & Refund Policy

Transparent, customer-friendly replacement and refund guidelines for all orders placed on PreTwin AI.

1. 7-Day Doorstep Replacement Guarantee

Eligible products delivered with defects, transit damage, missing accessories, or incorrect specifications can be replaced free of charge within 7 calendar days of delivery.

2. Order Cancellation Prior to Dispatch

You can cancel any pending order directly from your My Orders page prior to dispatch. When an order is cancelled:

  • Any redeemed PreTwin Reward Points are refunded back into your points ledger immediately.
  • Online payments (Razorpay/Cards/UPI) are reversed to the original source within 3–5 working days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) orders require zero cancellation fee.

3. Refund Processing Timelines

Payment Mode Refund Method Expected Turnaround
PreTwin Reward Points Points Ledger Credited Instant (Immediate)
UPI / Google Pay / PhonePe Original VPA / Bank Account 24–48 hours
Credit / Debit Cards Original Issuing Bank Card 3–5 business days
Cash on Delivery (COD) NEFT Bank Transfer / Store Credit 2–3 business days after pickup

4. How to Initiate a Return

Visit your Orders page, select the delivered order, and click "Request Return / Replacement". Or reach our 24x7 desk at Customer Service.
