🔒 PreTwin AI Privacy Policy

How we safeguard your shopping twin taste models, location data, and order credentials.

1. Overview

PreTwin Technologies Private Limited ("PreTwin", "we", "us") values your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you interact with PreTwin AI, your digital shopping twin, and our e-commerce platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password hashes (via secure bcrypt), and Google OAuth profile IDs when explicitly authenticated.
  • Shopping Preferences: Product categories, brand affinities, budget ceilings, and frequency indicators used exclusively to personalize your shopping twin.
  • Delivery Information: Pincode, city, state, and street address provided during checkout for order fulfillment.
  • Location Data: We never track GPS coordinates automatically in the background. Geolocation is requested only when you click "Use my current location", and manual city/pincode entry is always available.

3. How AI Models Use Your Data

Your personal twin predictions and reorder cycles are calculated in an isolated, privacy-preserving session. We do not sell your personal taste profiles or search queries to third-party ad brokers.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All transmissions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption. Payment transactions are processed directly via PCI-DSS compliant gateways (Razorpay) without storing your card CVVs or net banking credentials.

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

If you have questions regarding your data or wish to export/delete your twin profile, contact privacy@pretwin.ai.
