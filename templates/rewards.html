📜 PreTwin Rewards Rules & Verification Policy

  • Standard Earning: You earn 5 reward points for every eligible completed purchase.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): For COD orders, points are credited to your account only after doorstep delivery and payment confirmation. Unpaid orders do not earn advance points.
  • Clear Conversion Value: Exactly 100 points = ₹1.00 (so 5 points equals ₹0.05). Points are never inflated or treated as 1:1 rupees.
  • Checkout Redemption: Simply tick "Apply Points" during checkout to instantly deduct your rupee equivalent from the final bill.
  • Cancellations & Returns: If an order is cancelled, redeemed points are refunded back to your ledger, and awarded points are safely reversed.
