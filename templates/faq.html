❓ PreTwin AI — Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear, transparent answers about your AI Shopping Twin, reward points, savings plans, Cash on Delivery, and returns.

What is PreTwin AI and how does my personal shopping twin work?
PreTwin AI is an agentic shopping assistant that creates a personalized "twin" of your taste preferences, budget limits, and replenishment cycles. Instead of browsing endlessly, your twin predicts what you need ahead of time, alerts you to price drops, and checks your cart for accidental duplicates or missing essentials.
How do PreTwin Reward Points work and how can I redeem them?
PreTwin maintains a strict, transparent conversion: 100 Reward Points = ₹1.00 INR. Every new user receives 500 Welcome Bonus points (₹5.00). You earn 5 points on every verified order. You can convert points directly into store vouchers on the Reward Points page or tick "Apply Points" during checkout to instantly reduce your bill.
Can I pay with Cash on Delivery (COD)?
Yes! Cash on Delivery is supported for all eligible pincodes and orders under ₹50,000. For COD orders, payment is made directly to the delivery executive upon doorstep arrival. Reward points are credited once the delivery and payment are confirmed.
How does the Smart Shopping Card protect my cart?
The Smart Shopping Card monitors your cart in real time. It checks for duplicate items (e.g., ordering two chargers by mistake), suggests missing companion accessories (like screen guards for a phone), and calculates multi-item bundle discounts to save you money.
What is your return and refund policy?
PreTwin offers a 7-day doorstep replacement or refund guarantee on all Assured products. If you cancel an order before dispatch, any redeemed reward points are refunded back to your ledger immediately, and pre-paid amounts are reversed to your original payment method.
How does PreTwin handle my location and privacy?
We never request your GPS location automatically. You can choose to enter your delivery pincode manually or click "Use my current location" only when you want to. Your personal taste data is encrypted and used exclusively to power your personal shopping twin.
